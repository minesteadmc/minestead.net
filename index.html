    ABOUT

    Minestead is a vanilla survival multiplayer server on hard difficulty. We don't have any pay-to-win ranks, land claims, or teleporting.

    Join a town or venture into the wild, trade with players using diamonds, and build road networks to travel the world. We have a few quality of life tweaks:

    • View and manage all your lodestones or make them public with a compass.
    • Build with mini blocks from the stonecutter and get mob heads as loot.
    • Pick the perfect painting or quickly strip your logs in the stonecutter.
    • Make your base more peaceful by using a "silence" name tag on mobs.
    • Store even more items by always getting two shulker shells.

    RULES

    Before you can use blocks or items, you must read the rules at spawn.
    • 1. You must be 16 years or older to play.
    • 2. Everyone belongs here. Be kind: No hate, harassment, discrimination, slurs, or bullying ever! Keep it light, no heavy debate topics like politics or religion.
    • 3. No griefing or stealing. You may harvest and replant crops unless posted, but do not take items from containers. Do not use xray or other unfair advantages.
    • 4. TNT duping is allowed, but no other dupes including rails, carpets, sand, and gravel. Redstone and chunk loaders should be used sparingly and be lag friendly.
    • 5. PVP is enabled but you must ask before fighting and trapping.
    • 6. Keep chat clean. No spamming, excessive caps, or advertising other servers.
    Follow staff instructions. All bans are permanent.
